Repeat Playlist Player: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Repeat Playlist Player does not collect, transmit to any server, or share personal data.

The lists, repeat counts, speeds and settings you create are saved in your browser (local extension storage and, if you have browser sync turned on, your browser vendor's sync storage under your own account). The developer cannot see them.

The extension contacts only youtube.com: to read the public playlist or channel pages you ask it to load, and to play videos in YouTube's own player. It has no analytics, no advertising and no developer-run server.

Uninstalling the extension removes the locally stored data.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
